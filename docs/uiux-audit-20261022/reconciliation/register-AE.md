**Newly noticed while verifying**
- **AE-R1 (S3, Present in PROD/STG/RC; MISLEADING-SUCCESS; NEW-UNASSIGNED).** Settings success notices never display. Every save handler calls `showNotice("success", …)` and then `await loadSettings()`, and `loadSettings` runs `setNotice(null)` synchronously before its first await (`settings/page.tsx:945-946`; PROD `:957`).
