## Priority deep-dives

### FLT-01: status override (Confirmed, Critical)
- **Writers of `status_override_active = true`:**
  - `[vehicleId]` PATCH, on any `status` in the body (`route.ts:136`).
  - List PATCH, whenever the status changes (`vehicles/route.ts:322`). Setting **Available** also counts.
