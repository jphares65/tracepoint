**What F2 addressed (from `F2-fleet-authorization-decisions.md` and the defect-stage ledger row "Inspection eligibility, cost hiding, issue reporting | F2 | S15, S16"):**
- The F2 doc cites no OA IDs directly. Its scope is the residue B2 left of **FLT-14** (the inspection route admitted everyone when `inspection_role_codes` was empty, and the issue POST had no permission check, per R5 FLT-14) and **NEW-01** (field-write authorization mismatch between API and RLS).

- Issue reporting (F2 #4) now requires maintenance access or inspection eligibility. None of the D candidates is fixed by F2. F2 *raises the impact* of D-27 and causes D-R1.

**Merge candidates (same root cause):**
- **D-01 + D-02 + D-03:** the `tracepoint://`-only QR scheme with no web/HTTPS story, plus app-required copy. Fix them together.

**Newly noticed while verifying:**
- **D-R1 (S3, RC-introduced by F2, FAILED-OP / LOST-INPUT-adjacent):**
  - VP always shows "Quick Inspect" and "Report Issue" (`VP:73` header). The vehicle GET returns no eligibility flag (no `canInspect`/`canSubmit` anywhere in VP, QIP or `api/fleet/vehicles/[vehicleId]/route.ts`).
  - Under F2, a `view_fleet` user who is not on a non-empty `inspection_role_codes` list (for example command staff, or a mechanic not listed) can open the vehicle and fill in a whole inspection or issue, then gets 403 ("Your agency role or permissions do not allow…": `inspections/route.ts:60-67`, `work-orders/route.ts:44`).
  - In PROD the API admitted them.
  - Fix: return `canInspect` from `canSubmitFleetInspection` in the vehicle GET and hide or disable the buttons with an explanation. XS–S, no migration.
  - Test: route test (GET includes `canInspect=false` for an unlisted `view_fleet` user) plus a component test.
- **D-R2 (S3, RC-introduced by F3/F6a, OTHER-FUNCTIONAL; needs a runtime check):**
  - An attempted Available/Attention change during a hold (D-60) persists `status_override_active=true`. Holds also escalate already-overridden vehicles while leaving the override active (`fleet-server.ts` refresh: "the override stays active").
  - Once the hold is released by a clean inspection or a clearance, `refreshFleetVehicle` leaves an overridden Out of Service vehicle alone, and `clearSafetyHold` "sets no status". The vehicle can therefore stay Out of Service after release until a manager sets the status again.
  - The clean-inspection message would then say "Vehicle remains ready" (D-58).
  - This fails safe, but readiness becomes stale.
  - Fix: clear `status_override_active` when the server re-escalates for a hold, or when a hold is cleared. Owner decision on override semantics. Test: route test (held, override, clean inspection) gives the expected status.
- **D-14 correction:** on *create*, the typed Open Issues count is stored and never recomputed (`api/fleet/vehicles/route.ts:188`, no refresh in POST). Kept under D-14 rather than given a new ID.

**Where the "likely production" inference matters:**
- **Inventory (D-62/63/65/70):** absent in the PROD tree, present in STG and RC. If production actually runs a newer staging-line image than 564ff78, these defects are live. Severity assumes they ship with the RC.
- **D-01/D-02:** in PROD, native scanning of printed labels fails entirely (no `api/mobile`). In STG it gets 400. Only the RC resolves.
- **D-58:** the default-config contradiction (all-Pass inspection while an open critical WO keeps the vehicle OOS → "remains ready") already exists in PROD. The F3/F6a no-WO / automation-off variants exist only in RC.
- **D-27/D-28:** the production impact is lower (pre-F2 semantics: listed roles add eligibility, and an empty list admits every member in bridge mode). In RC, both decide who is *excluded* from inspecting.
